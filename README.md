# Snow Leap — Mini Runner

A small, dependency-free winter runner game made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Dodge icy logs, collect power-ups, and try to beat your best score.

## Run it in VS Code

1. Open the `game` folder in VS Code.
2. Open `index.html` to hit the snowy slopes.
3. If you have the **Live Server** extension installed, right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**.
4. Otherwise, open the `game` folder in File Explorer and double-click `index.html` to play in your browser.

No packages or build steps are needed.

## How to play

- Select **Start running** to begin.
- Press **Space**, **Arrow Up**, or **W** to jump.
- On a phone or tablet, tap the game area to jump.
- Choose **Easy**, **Normal**, or **Hard** before starting. Harder modes start faster and have tighter obstacle gaps.
- The runner speeds up as your score increases.
- Collect the glowing power-ups:
  - **↑ Flight** lets you fly over obstacles for a few seconds.
  - **◆ Shield** blocks one obstacle collision.
  - **✦ Gem** adds 50 points.
- Without a shield or flight, hitting a hazard ends the run. Choose **Run it back** (or press a jump key) to restart.

Your current score and best score appear above the game. The best score lasts until you close or refresh the page.
